# 🖼️ Gallery Management Instructions

## How to Manage Your Gallery Images

### **Adding New Images:**
1. **Go to WordPress Admin** → **Gallery Images** → **Add New**
2. **Set a Title** for your image (e.g., "Beach Sunset", "Mountain View")
3. **Upload Featured Image** - Click "Set featured image" and upload your photo
4. **Add Caption** (optional) - This will appear when hovering over the image
5. **Set Alt Text** (optional) - Important for accessibility
6. **Click "Publish"**

### **Reordering Images:**
1. **Go to WordPress Admin** → **Gallery Images**
2. **Drag and drop** images to reorder them
3. **The order number** shows the display sequence
4. **Lower numbers** appear first

### **Editing Images:**
1. **Click on any image title** in the Gallery Images list
2. **Change the title, caption, or alt text**
3. **Replace the featured image** if needed
4. **Click "Update"** to save changes

### **Removing Images:**
1. **Hover over an image** in the Gallery Images list
2. **Click "Trash"** to remove it
3. **Images go to trash** and can be restored if needed

### **Image Requirements:**
- **Recommended size**: 800x600 pixels or larger
- **File types**: JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP
- **File size**: Under 5MB for best performance
- **Aspect ratio**: Any ratio works (images will be cropped to fit)

### **Pro Tips:**
- **Use descriptive titles** for better organization
- **Add captions** to tell the story behind each image
- **Keep alt text concise** but descriptive
- **Order images** by importance or story flow

### **Need Help?**
- **Contact your web developer** for technical issues
- **Check WordPress documentation** for general help
- **Images update automatically** on your website after saving

---

**Remember**: Changes to your gallery appear immediately on your website!
